Health OS

A personal health system: strategy + tactics + data

github.com/nsshap/healthos

The idea

Two layers, grounded in Medicine 3.0 (Peter Attia) and exercise science:

Strategy The Board
Long-horizon risks, biomarkers, CMO directives, long-term goals.
Tactics The Field
Daily calories, training, sleep, logs — executing the plan.

Hierarchy: CMO directives > user preferences > default calculations.

How it runs in production

  • Telegram bot — main interface: commands and free-form chat.
  • OpenAI (GPT-4o) — replies and tool-calling (food logs, Oura, recipes, etc.).
  • Supabase — recipes, daily logs, Oura ring data, research_items table.
  • Health OS files — profile, directives, strategy, training program (YAML/Markdown in the repo).

Bot deploy: Railway (service runs python bot/bot.py).

Research Scout — why it exists

Automatically gather fresh material on sleep, metabolism, cardio, strength, and longevity; score relevance to your profile; and once a week deliver a summary digest in Russian.

  • Every day: crawl RSS → dedupe → model scoring → write to the database.
  • Once a week (Sunday): top articles from the last 7 days → synthesis in Telegram + source links.

How the research pipeline works

  1. Up to 10 newest entries per feed; items with a unique URL enter processing.
  2. Score 0–10 vs your profile (Medicine 3.0, sleep, metabolism, ApoB/VO2, strength, etc.).
  3. Stored in Supabase; Sunday digest includes items with score ≥ 6, top 7 for the narrative + links.
  4. Manual bot commands: /digest, /research, /research run.

Server schedule (UTC): daily scout ~05:00; Sunday digest ~10:00.
